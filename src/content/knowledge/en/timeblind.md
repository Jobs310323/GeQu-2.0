With ADHD, time isn't sensed well. There's only "now" and "not now", with a blank in between. That produces:

- A 20-minute task gets put off for a week because it feels enormous
- "Just five more minutes" turns into an hour and a half
- A deadline a month out doesn't register at all — until it's tomorrow

**What helps:**

- **Make time visible.** Not a mental count — a timer in view. Analog beats digital: you can see it running down.
- **Measure instead of guessing.** Time how long washing the dishes or answering that email actually takes. It's usually a fraction of what it felt like, and the dread of the task drops with it.
- **Break the future into pieces.** "Turn in the project at the end of the month" doesn't register. "Sketch the outline today at 3pm" does.
