title: Money and impulse spending
summary: Automation is more reliable than self-control.
minutes: 2
---

Impulse purchases are a direct result of weak inhibition, not carelessness. Time blindness adds to it by making it hard to feel the future consequences of today's spending.

**What works without leaning on self-control:**

- **A 24-hour pause** on anything above an amount you set yourself. The urge either sticks around or evaporates — usually the latter.
- **Remove saved cards** from stores and your browser. Every extra step between wanting and paying lowers the odds of buying.
- **Automatic transfers** to savings on payday. Money you don't see doesn't get spent.
- **A separate card for everyday spending**, with a set limit. A cap instead of a decision.
- **Reminders for recurring bills** — late payments with ADHD are usually about forgetting, not about not having the money.

Each of these replaces a decision you'd otherwise have to make in the moment of temptation.
