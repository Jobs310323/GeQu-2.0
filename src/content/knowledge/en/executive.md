title: Executive function: what's actually breaking down
summary: Six separate skills that usually get lumped together under the word "organized".
minutes: 3
---

"Disorganized" isn't one skill — it's at least six. It helps to know which of them are actually giving you trouble.

1. **Inhibition** — the ability to not react instantly. This is where interrupting people and impulse purchases come from.
2. **Working memory** — holding several things in mind at once. This is where "walked into the room and forgot why" comes from.
3. **Switching** — moving from task to task without losing your place. This is where getting stuck and struggling to exit hyperfocus come from.
4. **Emotional regulation** — returning to baseline after a spike.
5. **Initiation** — starting without an external push. The single most common bottleneck.
6. **Planning** — breaking something big into steps and estimating the time.

**Why this matters.** Strategies work when they target a specific function. If initiation is the weak point, to-do lists won't help — shrinking the first step will. If working memory is the weak point, writing it down helps — willpower doesn't.

Pick out the two functions that trip you up most often, and choose your techniques around those.
