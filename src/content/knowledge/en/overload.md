The filter on incoming signals runs weaker with ADHD. An open office, background music, bright light, a conversation in the next room — all of it comes in at once, unfiltered.

Overload feels like irritation with no clear cause, a wish for everyone to just stop talking, or sudden blankness — thoughts stop connecting.

**First aid:**

- Go somewhere quiet and dim for 10 minutes, close your eyes
- Breathe with a long exhale — the exhale switches the nervous system into recovery mode
- Noise-cancelling headphones, even without music playing

**Prevention:** notice your own triggers and remove them ahead of time. This isn't being precious — it's environment tuning, the same as glasses for near-sightedness.
