Physical activity is one of the few things that reliably changes your state, within half an hour. Afterward, starting usually gets easier and holding attention gets easier too.

**Practical uses:**

- **20 minutes before a hard task** — not for fitness, for state.
- **Two minutes when you're stuck.** Push-ups, a walk up the stairs, a stretch. Often that's enough to come unstuck.
- **Consistency beats intensity.** Three short sessions a week give more than one exhausting one.

Don't wait for motivation. The same rule applies here: action first, mood second. "Dynamics" shows what your mood looks like on days you trained.
