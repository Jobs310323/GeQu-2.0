Hunger and dehydration hit attention harder than people tend to think, and ADHD makes them easy to miss — the body's signals don't come through well, especially in hyperfocus.

**Three things worth the effort:**

- **Don't skip breakfast with protein.** Coffee on an empty stomach gives a lift and, an hour later, a crash that's easy to mistake for "just not my day."
- **Water within reach.** Dehydration shows up as fatigue and brain fog. A bottle on the desk solves it without needing willpower.
- **Respect caffeine.** It genuinely helps, but after 2pm it wrecks tonight's sleep — and tomorrow, bad sleep takes more than today's coffee gave.

This isn't about eating right in general. It's three levers with a noticeable effect on attention.
