Many people with ADHD describe an intensely painful reaction to criticism, rejection, or even the suspicion of either. A neutral "we need to talk" triggers a wave out of proportion to the trigger.

Important: the reaction feels instant and physical — this isn't "making a big deal out of nothing," and it isn't a choice.

**What helps:**

- **Name what's happening.** "I'm getting hit by a rejection reaction right now" already lowers the intensity, because it moves it from a feeling into an observation.
- **Delay your reply by 20 minutes.** The peak passes quickly. Almost everything people later regret sending was sent inside that window.
- **Separate the fact from the interpretation.** Fact: "they said redo the section." Interpretation: "they think I'm incompetent." Working through a thought in the CBT section is exactly for this.
- **Collect counter-evidence ahead of time.** A list of things that went well — to read during the wave, not compose in the middle of it.
