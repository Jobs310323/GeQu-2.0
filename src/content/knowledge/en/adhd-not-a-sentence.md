title: ADHD isn't a life sentence
summary: What comes bundled in besides the difficulties.
minutes: 2
---

With the right approach — medication, cognitive behavioural therapy, structure and habits — people with ADHD reach remarkable heights.

Creativity, the capacity for hyperfocus on things that interest you, unconventional thinking, and energy are the strengths that come bundled with ADHD. The key is learning to manage the weak points.

In practice, that comes down to three things:

- **Don't fix your character — change your environment.** A timer in view, fixed spots for things, reminders instead of relying on memory.
- **Lean on your strengths instead of levelling out your weaknesses.** Tasks that need speed and unconventional thinking usually go better than the rest.
- **Don't carry it alone.** A doctor, therapy, and understanding people around you change the trajectory more than any organizational trick.
