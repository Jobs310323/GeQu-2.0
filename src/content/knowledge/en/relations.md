title: How to explain it to the people close to you
summary: So it doesn't sound like an excuse.
minutes: 2
---

From the outside, ADHD traits read as attitude: not listening means it doesn't matter; forgetting means it wasn't important; interrupting means no respect. That's what hurts the people close to you — not the traits themselves.

**What helps in the conversation:**

- **Explain the mechanism, don't apologize for it.** "I forget things not because I don't care, but because I don't hold onto it — so please send it as a message instead of mentioning it in passing."
- **Offer a fix, not a request for patience.** A shared calendar, reminders, an agreement that "important things go in writing."
- **Acknowledge the impact.** The mechanism explains it, but doesn't erase that the other person was hurt. One doesn't replace the other.
- **Agree on a signal.** A word the other person can use to say "you're interrupting" without it turning into a fight.

The goal is to move the problem from "what's wrong with you" to "how do we set this up" — where you're both already on the same side.
