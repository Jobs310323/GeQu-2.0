A to-do list shows **what** needs doing but stays silent on **when**. With time blindness, that leaves the hardest decision for the exact moment when deciding is hardest.

Time blocks remove that choice: "10:00–11:00, I write the report" instead of "write the report."

**How to do it in a livable way:**

- **Budget one and a half times the time you think you'll need.** Almost everyone with ADHD systematically underestimates.
- **Leave empty blocks.** A day with no slack falls apart at the first delay.
- **No more than three important blocks a day.** Everything else is a bonus.
- **Set blocks around your energy**, not your calendar's convenience: hard things at your peak, mechanical things at your dip.

A plan that falls apart by lunch is fine. A bad plan is one that never left room for that.
