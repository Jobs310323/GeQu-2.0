title: How ADHD affects daily life
summary: Work, relationships, home life, money, and mental health.
minutes: 3
---

**Work and school.** Trouble with long-term projects, frequent job changes from boredom or conflict with management, deadline "firefighting" — the classic all-nighter the night before something's due.

**Relationships.** Emotional swings (rejection-sensitive dysphoria), sudden flashes of anger or tears. A partner may feel unheard or unvalued.

**Home and finances.** Clutter piling up, bills paid late, impulse spending, a pot forgotten on the stove.

**Mental health.** Anxiety, depression, sleep problems, and dependencies (alcohol, gaming, social media) are frequent companions of ADHD — the brain looks for fast dopamine wherever it's easiest to find.

Almost every item on this list isn't a life sentence — it's the consequence of a specific mechanism that has a workaround. The rest of this knowledge base is about those workarounds.
