ADHD procrastination is almost never about "not wanting to do it." It's about the **starting barrier**: moving from rest into action needs a dopamine kick that isn't showing up.

The good news: the barrier only stands at the entrance. Two or three minutes into the work, it's gone.

**Techniques that go around the entrance:**

- **The five-minute rule.** Agree to do exactly five minutes, and give yourself honest permission to stop after. You usually won't want to.
- **A ridiculously small step.** Not "write the report" — "open the document and type the title."
- **A starting ritual.** The same little sequence before working — pour water, put on headphones, start the timer. Your brain starts reading it as a cue.
- **Don't start from zero.** When you finish for the day, leave the next step half-done. Coming back to something started is far easier than starting cold.
