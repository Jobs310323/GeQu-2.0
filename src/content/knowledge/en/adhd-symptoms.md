title: Core symptoms and how they show up
summary: What inattention, impulsivity, and hyperactivity look like in everyday life.

---

Symptoms are usually split into two groups. Either can predominate in a given person, or both together.

**Inattention**

- Difficulty holding attention on boring or routine tasks
- Chronic procrastination
- Forgetfulness: lost keys, lost phones, forgotten promises
- Losing the thread of a conversation, "checking out" mid-conversation
- Trouble organizing time and space (clutter)

**Impulsivity and hyperactivity**

- Impatience: hard to wait your turn
- Interrupting, blurting out an answer before the question is finished
- Inner restlessness (a feeling of being "driven by a motor")
- A pull toward risky behavior (fast driving, impulse purchases)
- Trouble filtering thoughts — saying whatever comes to mind

Worth remembering: everyone has one item from this list sometimes. It's called a disorder when the traits are persistent, trace back to childhood, and noticeably interfere across several areas of life at once.
