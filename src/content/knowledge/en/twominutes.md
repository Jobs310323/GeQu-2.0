---
category: life
title: The two-minute rule
summary: What to do with the stream of little things.
minutes: 1
---

If something takes less than two minutes, do it right away — don't write it down.

The reason isn't discipline, it's arithmetic: adding a task to a list, remembering it, coming back, and doing it takes longer together than the task itself. And every postponed little thing takes up a piece of working memory while it waits.

**Watch for the trap:** the rule works for a stream of small things, but turns into avoidance when two-minute tasks get done instead of the main one. If you sit down for something important, do the important thing first — small stuff after.

A good time for the two-minute rule is the end of the workday.
