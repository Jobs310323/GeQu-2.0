A long task list works against you with ADHD: your brain sees the volume, can't pick a priority, and shuts down. By evening, seven of twenty things are done — and it feels like failure, even though seven things is a lot.

**The rule:** pick exactly three tasks each day.

- **One main task** — the one that makes the day count if it gets done
- **Two supporting tasks** — important, but not critical

Everything else lives in the general list and stays out of view. Finish the three, and the day is closed. Energy left over? Take more, but as a bonus, not a debt.

Three isn't about low expectations. It's about ending the evening looking at a plan you completed, instead of one you didn't. That sense of completion is fuel for tomorrow.
