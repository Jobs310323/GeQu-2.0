Hyperfocus is the flip side of attention trouble: a state where everything else disappears. That's a genuine advantage, but it has a cost — you can't leave it on demand, and it doesn't pick useful things to lock onto.

**How to use it:**

- Plan demanding work for when hyperfocus tends to happen (for most people that's morning or late evening — check your own "Dynamics")
- Clear away anything that could hijack your focus **before** you go in

**How to leave without losses:**

- An external alarm across the room — an internal timer won't cut through
- Put water within reach beforehand: your body will remind you before your head does
- Write down where you stopped while you're still inside the state — you won't remember it after

Hyperfocus isn't the enemy. The only danger is not noticing where it went.
