title: A home that doesn't run on willpower
summary: Order comes from how the space is set up, not from character.
minutes: 2
---

Storage systems that need tidiness fall apart in a week with ADHD. What works is a system where putting something away is **easier** than dropping it.

**Principles:**

- **One step instead of three.** An open bin instead of a drawer with a lid. A hook instead of a hanger in a closet.
- **Visible means it exists.** Clear containers, open shelves. Put away in an opaque box, and it stops existing.
- **A fixed spot for anything critical.** Keys, wallet, phone, documents — one place, always. This is the one thing worth the effort of sticking to.
- **An entry point.** A shelf by the door that catches everything brought in. Sorted once a day, and nothing gets lost.

If the system breaks, don't blame yourself — simplify it by one more step.
