An open task occupies part of working memory until it's written down or done. With ADHD, working memory is narrow to begin with, so ten "don't forget"s in your head eat up nearly all of it — hence the background tension and the sense that your head is buzzing.

**The practice: a brain dump.**

1. Set a timer for 10 minutes
2. Write down everything circling in your head, no sorting, no judging
3. Cross out anything that genuinely isn't your responsibility
4. From what's left, pick three things for today; the rest goes to Kanban

The point isn't the list — it's that your brain stops having to hold on. You can even skip reopening the list afterward; the tension drops anyway.

Do this once a week, and any time you feel overloaded.
